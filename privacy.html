Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how PFR Capital ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application PFR Capital (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Data You Provide

PFR Capital is designed to operate locally on your device. When you use the App, the financial information you enter — including transactions, budgets, investments, categories, account names, and receipt photos — is saved locally on your device.

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect personal identifying information such as your name, email address, phone number, or date of birth in order to use the App.

1.2 Automatically Collected Data

The App does not include third-party analytics, tracking SDKs, or advertising frameworks. The only information automatically collected is non-personal technical data that may be logged by your device's operating system, which is outside our control.

1.3 Receipt Photos

If you choose to attach a receipt photo to a transaction, the image is stored locally on your device. Photos are never uploaded to our servers because we do not operate servers that receive your data.

2. How We Use Your Information

Because your data is stored locally on your device, we do not access, view, transmit, or process your financial entries. The App uses the data only to:

  • Display your dashboard, reports, and transaction history.
  • Compute balances, budgets, and investment performance locally.
  • Persist your entries between app sessions on your device.

3. Data Storage and Security

All financial data and receipt images are stored locally on your device using standard iOS data protection mechanisms. We recommend that you protect your device with a passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID, and keep your iOS software up to date. While we work to protect your information, no method of storage is completely immune to unauthorized access.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share your personal or financial data with third parties. Because we do not collect such data on our servers, there is no central database from which data could be disclosed.

5. Children's Privacy

PFR Capital is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can address the concern.

6. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with third-party analytics, advertising, or social media services. The App does not connect to external financial institutions or pull information from your bank accounts.

7. Your Choices and Controls

  • Access and edit: You can view, edit, or delete any entry directly within the App at any time.
  • Delete your data: Deleting the App from your device removes all locally stored financial data and receipt photos.
  • Reset: The App provides a sample data seeder and the ability to start fresh from settings.

8. International Users

PFR Capital is offered globally through the Apple App Store. Because the App does not transmit your data to us, the location from which you use the App does not affect how we handle your information. However, you are responsible for ensuring that your use of the App complies with the laws of your jurisdiction.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Your continued use of the App after a change indicates that you accept the updated policy.
